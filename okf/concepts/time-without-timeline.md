The hardest part of the [pivot](/concepts/node-graph.md), resolved by one distinction.

# Time ≠ timeline

- **Time** is the dimension audio lives in. It cannot be removed — a sound *is* a function
  of time. Reverb, delay, a note's attack are all time.
- **The timeline** is a *UI metaphor*: a horizontal ruler you place clips on. Optional.
  FL/LMMS/Logic chose it; Max / Pure Data / Reaktor / VCV / modular synths did not.

**Going node-based deletes the arrangement timeline and lets time be *generated by
nodes*.** A **clock node** emits pulses; a **sequencer node** advances on each pulse and
**wraps**. Time becomes a *signal that flows through the patch*, not a global axis you draw
on. (Decided with the author 2026-06-29: keep time-via-nodes; do not go fully continuous —
integer [ticks](/concepts/note-as-rune.md) stay, for clean triplets/quantization and MIDI
interop.)

# Ticks survive — repurposed

Ticks felt restrictive as a *global coordinate system*; as the **output of a clock node**
they are composable and relative. `model.py`'s tick↔second helpers carry over unchanged —
they convert a clock/sequencer node's local ticks to seconds at the
[render boundary](/concepts/render-domain.md).

# What a loop becomes

A **clock → sequencer that wraps** (a phasor / modular counter). A drum loop = clock →
drum-sequencer (wraps every 16 steps) → sampler → output; it just *runs*. Layering
("a drum loop underneath") = several sequencer chains summed at a mix node sharing one
clock. Looping becomes the *default*; a fixed-length song is the special case (a clock
gated to run once). See [loops & feedback](/concepts/loops-and-feedback.md).

# MIDI stays; "mini-timelines" live inside nodes (dev-answer 2, 2026-07-06)

The author's grounding: **we are getting rid of timelines, not of MIDI.** MIDI is the
near-universal, irreducibly *time-based* substrate of music production — so time-ordered
note data survives as **mini-timelines inside nodes**: a sequencer node's contents (its
[note-runes](/concepts/note-as-rune.md)) are a small, local, wrapping timeline, arranged
in a piano-roll *inside that node* ([own UI](/components/own-ui.md)). The global ruler is
gone; the local one is a node's *content*. This is the same distinction as above — time
as signal between nodes, time as ordered content within one — and a **MIDI file** is
simply an importable serialization of such content (see the sequence-node direction in
[node types](/concepts/node-types.md)).

# Status

**`current` (2026-07-08).** Looping is the shipped default: a sequencer/sequence's
`length`/`offset`/`repeat` loop its figure to fill the output node's `duration`
window (repeat unset = fill; one-shot = 1) — `render._expand_sequence`, proven by ear
in `tests/test_prototype.py` (criteria 3/4/6). Mini-timelines live inside nodes (the
piano roll edits them); MIDI files import at project tempo. The clock *signal* itself
remains descriptive until block-streaming playback (the trivial backend renders
whole windows; the LMMS engine step makes clock pulses literal).
